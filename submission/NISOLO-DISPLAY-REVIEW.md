No affirmative permission for this public replay was established. Current official
terms were accessible at https://nisolo.com/policies/terms-of-service (page last
updated January 21, 2025). Ownership of Proprietary Rights reserves website content
and trademark rights. Limited License permits limited personal/educational/

restrict mirroring and automated copying/access. This is not a blanket license to
redistribute 48 screenshots on GitHub, host the full captured sequence, or publish
it in a prize-entry promotional video. It is also not a finding that this use is
necessarily unlawful; applicability/enforceability and legal exceptions need their
own assessment. We did not establish what terms applied at original capture.

## Possible exception is not a confirmed clearance

US copyright fair use can cover criticism, research and commentary. Here, the
agent-behavior analysis adds a purpose beyond selling shoes and does not provide
checkout; however, a prize-entry/public promotional context, 48 downloadable
captures and full product imagery complicate the assessment. Attribution, a
non-endorsement label, cropping or calling it educational does not automatically
clear copyright, trademark, contract or competition requirements.
https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/ explains the case-specific four-factor analysis.
No definitive fair-use determination is claimed.

The hackathon video requirements separately require permission for included
third-party marks/copyrighted material, and project entries must respect third-
party rights: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules (sections 4 and 7).
Do not treat the existence of a possible legal exception as verified organizer
acceptance of this particular video.
